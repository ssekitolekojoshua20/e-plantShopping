# This is the README.md file for the **e-plantShopping**

The Paradise Nursery Shopping Application is here to help farmers shop for their preferred type of plant online.
You place your orders online and they are delivered without you needing to move.
we provide you with a wide range of plant types for you to choose from.

How to navigate it;
you select the plant you want by clicking on the increment buttons to add the plant in the cart and 
after selecting all plants you want click the cart icon to see your total cost then you can decide to continue shopping
by clicking the continue shopping button or checkout button when you are done
